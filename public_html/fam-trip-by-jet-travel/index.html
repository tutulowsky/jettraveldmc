FAM trip by Jet Travel DMC

The best way to understand a destination is to experience it first-hand.

Together with #VisualEuropeGroup, we welcomed 9 international event organisers and corporate travel managers to Budapest and the surrounding region, where they enjoyed an unforgettable FAM trip.

During this inspiring trip, we showcased recent developments, explored hotels and event venues, and gave participants a taste of the many incentive programmes our destination has to offer, all while enjoying delicious Hungarian cuisine.

At Jet Travel, we are always happy to design and support inspiring MICE activities — 365 days a year.
